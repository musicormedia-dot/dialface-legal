DialFace: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

DialFace ("the app") is an iPhone app developed by Music or Media Holding ("we", "us") that lets you call the people you choose by tapping their photo. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Information we collect

DialFace does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising. We never receive the names, numbers, or photos you add.

People you add

The names, phone numbers, photos, and calling preferences (Phone, WhatsApp, voice note or FaceTime), and your display settings that you add are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are deleted when you remove a person or delete the app.

Contacts

When you use "Fill from Contacts", iOS shows its own contact picker. DialFace receives only the single contact you select (its name, phone numbers, and photo) and saves just what you keep, on your device. The app does not read or upload your address book.

Photos

When you choose a photo for a person, iOS shows its own photo picker. DialFace receives only the photo you select and stores a copy on your device. The app does not access the rest of your photo library.

Making calls and voice notes

When you tap a photo, DialFace passes that person's phone number to Apple's Shortcuts app on your iPhone, which places the call through the Phone app or WhatsApp. For a WhatsApp voice note, DialFace opens the person's chat in WhatsApp. For FaceTime, DialFace hands the number to Apple's FaceTime. All of this happens on your device; we never receive the number. WhatsApp and FaceTime are also subject to their own privacy policies.

Purchases

The free month and the one-time unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, name, or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether the free month or the unlock is active on your device.

Feedback emails

If you choose "Send a Suggestion" or "Report a Problem", your own Mail app opens a message to us. A problem report includes the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language, to help us fix issues. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself, and we use it only to reply and improve the app.

Children's privacy

DialFace is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.
