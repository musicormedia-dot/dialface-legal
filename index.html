DialFace: Support

Call the people you love with one tap on their photo.

What DialFace does

DialFace shows the people you call most as big photos. Tap a photo and DialFace starts the call the way you chose for that person:

  • Phone call: one tap, no "Call this number?" question.
  • WhatsApp call: one tap, no question.
  • WhatsApp voice note: opens the person's WhatsApp chat, ready for you to hold the microphone and record.
  • FaceTime Audio or Video: iPhone asks you to confirm each FaceTime call (this is an Apple rule).

Press and hold a photo to call once in a different way, or to edit the person.

Getting started

The first time you open DialFace, a short guide asks what you want to use ("I want to use...") and helps you add only the shortcuts you need to Apple's Shortcuts app. You do this once. You can open the guide again any time: tap the gear button, then Calling Options & Setup.

Free month and unlock

DialFace is free to download. Every feature is free for 30 days. To start the free month, Apple shows its own confirmation screen; nothing is charged, now or later, and there is no subscription.

When the month ends, you can choose to unlock DialFace forever with a single one-time purchase. Nothing is ever charged automatically. With Family Sharing, your family can use the unlock too. To see your days left or unlock, tap the gear button and look under DialFace. If you change iPhone, tap Restore Purchase.

Make it yours

  • Photo size: Large, Medium or Small (about 8 to 24 people per page).
  • Name under photo: full name, first name, or photos only.
  • Background: a ready-made style, any colour, or your own photo.
  • Photos: choose from Photos or Contacts. DialFace centres the face automatically, and you can move and scale it.

Questions

Why does DialFace use Shortcuts?

Normally, iPhone asks "Call this number?" every time an app starts a call. DialFace works with Apple's free Shortcuts app so that one tap on a photo starts the call straight away.

I tap a photo and nothing happens, or Shortcuts shows an error

The shortcuts are probably missing or renamed. Tap the gear, then Calling Options & Setup, and add them again. They must be named exactly "DialFace Call" and "DialFace WhatsApp".

I see "DialFace Call 1" in Shortcuts

The shortcut was added twice. Delete the extra copy and make sure one is named exactly "DialFace Call".

iPhone asks for permission when I call

This happens only the first time. Tap "Always Allow" and iPhone will not ask again.

WhatsApp calls or voice notes don't work

Check that WhatsApp is installed, that the person uses WhatsApp, and that their number in DialFace includes the country code (for example +961...).

iPhone asks which app to use for WhatsApp

If both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business are installed, iPhone may ask once which one to open. Choose the one you use.

FaceTime asks me to confirm every call

That is an Apple rule for FaceTime and cannot be turned off. Phone and WhatsApp calls start with one tap.

How do I edit or remove someone?

Press and hold their photo and choose Edit. To reorder or delete people, tap the gear, then Edit.

How do I get a full-screen photo during a normal call?

The call screen itself is shown by iPhone. To see a full-screen photo, open the person in the Contacts app, tap Edit, then Contact Photo & Poster, and set a poster.
