DialFace: Support

Call the people you love with one tap on their photo.

Getting started

The first time you open DialFace, a short guide helps you add two small shortcuts to Apple's Shortcuts app. You only do this once. You can open the guide again any time from the app's Settings (the gear button) under "Shortcut Setup Guide".

Questions

Why does DialFace use Shortcuts?

Normally, iPhone asks "Call this number?" every time an app starts a call. DialFace works with Apple's Shortcuts app so that one tap on a photo starts the call straight away.

I tap a photo and nothing happens, or Shortcuts shows an error

The shortcuts are probably missing or renamed. Open DialFace, tap the gear, then "Shortcut Setup Guide", and add them again. The shortcuts must be named exactly "DialFace Call" and "DialFace WhatsApp".

I see "DialFace Call 1" in Shortcuts

The shortcut was added twice. Delete the extra copy and make sure one is named exactly "DialFace Call".

iPhone asks for permission when I call

This happens only the first time. Tap "Always Allow" and iPhone will not ask again.

WhatsApp calls don't work

Check that WhatsApp is installed, that the person uses WhatsApp, and that their number in DialFace includes the country code (for example +961...).

How do I edit or remove someone?

Press and hold their photo and choose Edit. To reorder or delete people, open Settings (the gear) and tap Edit.
